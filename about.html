A full-service marine company you can trust

Aquamarine Construction, Inc. is a full-service company offering a wide variety of marine services — dock construction and repair, boat lifts and repair, seawall repairs, dredging, boat repairs, and much more. Our highly skilled team has more than 40 years of marine contractor experience and commercial-grade equipment.

Our primary goal is to provide each valued client with exceptional customer service and the high-quality workmanship that is our hallmark. In fact, we hold an A+ rating on the Better Business Bureau website and have thousands of satisfied customers — with thousands of referrals available upon request.

How we build

Review

Free, interactive design review

We start with a free review — a primer on construction codes, build zones, boat preferences, decking materials, and how you want to use the space — so the plan fits your exact goals.

Design

Plan-view drawing & quote

We design a plan-view drawing to review with you, accompanied by a quote. From there we modify as desired and review any cost changes those changes might drive.

Build

Commercial-grade construction

Our crew uses commercial-grade barges and cranes — not homemade machines — so we can back new builds with a 2-year written warranty. The same care applies to repairs of any size.
