AQUAMARINE CONSTRUCTION WEBSITE: HOW TO UPLOAD
================================================

Everything in this folder IS the website. Upload all of it, exactly as it is.

WHAT'S IN HERE
--------------
Pages (.html)      index, about, services, gallery, reviews, contact,
                   boat-docks, boat-dock-repair, boat-lifts, seawall-repair,
                   tiki-huts, kayak-stations, marine-services,
                   privacy, terms, thankyou
Styles & scripts   style.css, gallery.css, script.js, gallery.js
Thankyou/          thank-you page (keep as is)
images/            all photos, sorted by type:
   site/              logo, favicon, video thumbnails
   docks/             dock photos
   boat-lifts/        boat lifts, lift covers, piling lifts
   tiki-huts/         tiki hut photos
   seawalls/          seawall photos
   kayak/             kayak & canoe lifts
   featured-project/  the tiki hut + canopy lift + dock project (testimonial video)

GITHUB
------
1. Open the repository on github.com (the main page, not inside a folder).
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
3. Open this folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it (Ctrl+A)
   and drag it into the upload box. The "images" folder keeps its subfolders.
4. Click "Commit changes". Wait 1-2 minutes, then refresh the site.

Do NOT upload this folder itself (that creates a folder inside the repository).
Upload what is INSIDE it.

HOSTINGER (aquamarinecapecoral.com)
-----------------------------------
1. File Manager > public_html.
2. Upload everything inside this folder, replacing the old files.

VIDEOS
------
The videos (video/andy-rich.mp4, video/testimonial.mp4) are not in this folder
because they are too large. They are already on the site. Leave the existing
"video" folder where it is.
