Tiki Huts

Custom, Code-Built Tiki Huts

It took us years to find the perfect team to do custom tiki huts. Ours are all engineered, permitted, and built to required codes from natural materials brought out from native habitat in the swamps, then stained and preserved against fungus and insects and installed the old-fashioned way for a rain-tight, beautiful, natural hut.

We build them in all sizes and shapes — round, rectangular, square, trapezoidal, no problem — and can add custom tables and other touches. We also repair any natural hut, from re-thatch to rebuild or expand.
