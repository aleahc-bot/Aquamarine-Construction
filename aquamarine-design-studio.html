A little about us

Full-service marine construction, built on the water

Aquamarine Construction, Inc. offers dock building, dock demolition, boat lift services and repairs, seawall repairs, dredging, boat repairs, and more.

Your dock is far more than a place for your vessel — it's a property enhancement, a secure shelter, and often a lively entertainment hub. We help you create a robust, attractive, highly functional dock that showcases your passion for the water.

Our team has more than 40 years of marine contractor experience, proudly operating under the Aquamarine name since 2002.

How we work

Every dock is built custom to your needs and budget

01

Review

A free, interactive review and design session — covering construction codes, build zones, boat preference, decking materials, and how you'll use the dock.

02

Design

We draw a plan-view drawing to review with you, paired with a quote. From there we modify as desired and review any cost changes together.

03

Build

Our crew uses commercial-grade barges and cranes — not homemade machines — so we back our work with a 2-year written warranty.
